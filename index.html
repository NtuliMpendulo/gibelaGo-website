Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about GibelaGo

How do I book a ride?

+

Download the GibelaGo app, sign up with your phone number, enter your destination, and tap "Request Ride". A nearby driver will accept your ride within seconds.

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept multiple payment methods including credit/debit cards, mobile money, and cash. You can add your preferred payment method in the app settings.

Are GibelaGo rides insured?

+

Yes, all GibelaGo rides are covered by comprehensive insurance. This protects both riders and drivers in case of any incident during the ride.

How are fares calculated?

+

Fares are calculated based on distance, time, and demand. You'll see the estimated fare before confirming your ride. There are no hidden charges.

What if I need to cancel my ride?

+

You can cancel your ride anytime before the driver arrives without any charge. If you cancel after the driver has started heading to you, a small cancellation fee may apply.

How do I become a GibelaGo driver?

+

Visit our website and click "Become a Driver". Fill out the application form, submit your documents, and wait for verification (usually 2-3 days). Once approved, download the driver app and start earning!

What is your customer support availability?

+

We offer 24/7 customer support through the app, email, and phone. You can reach us anytime you need assistance.

Is my personal information safe?

+

Yes, we use industry-leading encryption and security measures to protect your personal information. Your phone number is never shared with drivers or riders without your consent.
